# 1990–2004 curation review

Four kairolexica contain 26 accepted entries: information-highway rhetoric (4),
affirmative cyber formations (9), reengineering evangelism (3), and dot-com
valuation/commercial exceptionalism (10). Accepted records use the shared schema.
No quotas were met by admitting generic business vocabulary.

These sources were actually opened through the web tool. Lurkertech and Spektrum
were additionally fetched through the configured environment proxy. Wikipedia
requests returned HTTP 403; no accepted record relies on an inaccessible
Wikipedia article. Retrieved research pages remain temporary, outside the repo.

## Dating limits

All signatures have medium confidence and estimated terminal fades. This is
intentional: documented backlash is not a measured year of zero usage. Cultural
onsets sometimes differ from first lexical appearances. Every annual peak in
these files is a curation hypothesis unless directly supported by the highway
counts. Sparse profiles are preferable to false precision.

Several entries isolate a bounded *sense*: disintermediation as a sweeping
internet promise, mind share/stickiness/eyeballs as speculative valuation, and
blank sheet of paper as radical reengineering doctrine. Their neutral or modern
senses are not claimed to have disappeared. This is an inclusion interpretation
that Tom should review if a stricter surface-form-only policy is intended.

Constructed usages are explicitly labeled constructed; they are examples for
intent retrieval, not historical quotations. Three additional short fragments
are attested and sourced. No quotation was invented.

## Coverage

The files provide support from 1990 through 2004. Reengineering is deliberately
small. Coverage after the bubble's collapse is thinner and depends on the
estimated declining signatures. No invented 2001–2004 fad was added to obscure
that deficiency.
