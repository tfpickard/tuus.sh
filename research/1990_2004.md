affirmative cyber formations (9), reengineering evangelism (3), and dot-com

## Dating limits

All signatures have medium confidence and estimated terminal fades. This is
intentional: documented backlash is not a measured year of zero usage. Cultural
onsets sometimes differ from first lexical appearances. Every annual peak in
these files is a curation hypothesis unless directly supported by the highway
counts. Sparse profiles are preferable to false precision.

Several entries isolate a bounded *sense*: disintermediation as a sweeping
internet promise, mind share/stickiness/eyeballs as speculative valuation, and
blank sheet of paper as radical reengineering doctrine. Their neutral or modern
senses are not claimed to have disappeared. This is an inclusion interpretation
that Tom should review if a stricter surface-form-only policy is intended.

Constructed usages are explicitly labeled constructed; they are examples for
intent retrieval, not historical quotations. Three additional short fragments
are attested and sourced. No quotation was invented.

## Context compatibility gate

All 16 retained entries now declare lexical `context_terms` for their attested
senses. Cyber commercial terms require purchase, shopping, ordering, or sales
contexts; network metaphors require network or digital contexts; reengineering
rhetoric requires work-process, organizational-redesign, or automation contexts.
Dot-com entries distinguish business exceptionalism and scaling, attention and
audience valuation, repeat-use retention, transaction coordination, remote
coordination, and removal of intermediaries. The terms are retrieval matching
aids, not additional historical evidence or user controls.

There are no genuinely context-independent discourse markers in this retained
subset. No functions, temporal signatures, canonical forms, variants, source
references, or examples were changed. Runtime fallback can choose nearby
context-compatible material when these period-specific technical senses do not
fit an ordinary request. Candidate records remain unchanged.

## Intent-specific coverage: mass-mediated formulas

network, commerce and process-redesign vocabulary cannot express an unrelated
request or thanks merely because it is nearby in time. A new, separately sourced
kairolexicon, `era_1990_mass_cultural_formulas`, accepts four formulas. No existing
corpus record changed. This raises the owned accepted set to **20 entries across
five kairolexica**; the full corpus validated at 113 entries across 18 kairolexica
at this check.

Direct scans of the 1991 Baltimore article and the 1995/1996 talk-to-the-hand
attestations could not be inspected. Sources and entry rationales identify these
as transcribed or reported primary material, never as independently viewed
scans. Independently inspected contemporary newspaper prose and the institutional
annual lists supply each record's retained attested example. Constructed complete
requests and thanks are labeled constructed, preserving formula meaning without
claiming that their newly assembled wording appeared historically.

Every peak and fade remains an estimate with medium confidence. Banishment is
contemporary backlash evidence, not a frequency measurement or proof of literal
extinction; later references and residual speech are expected. The corpus covers
the expanded praise formula, the specific da spelling, and the standalone
skeptical exclamation, not older or continuing related words. Origins in Black
vernacular are not dated to first mainstream appropriation. The signatures model
the bounded mass-mediated cultural moment rather than claiming an invention date.

These broad formulas have no technical context prerequisites. The two dismissive
markers are not licensed for praise, gratitude, neutral information or deferral.
Their sharpness is part of their attested meaning, not a configurable tone.
`my bad` and `whatever` were rejected from this addition because a banishment
nomination alone does not distinguish their continuing ordinary use from a clean
flare-and-die trajectory. `you go, girl` has a short documented uptake-to-backlash
interval, but was not included in this generic gap repair because it addresses a
gender-specific recipient. No speculative variants were added.


## Explicit politeness and usage-role gate

The v5 generation review demonstrated that a dismissive refusal cannot satisfy
an explicitly polite speaking-invitation decline. Both `as_if_formula` and
`talk_to_hand` now exclude intents containing polite, politely, respectful,
respectfully, courteous or courteously. This is a sense-compatibility constraint
on a stated intent, not a new user control. Their existing declining function
and historical evidence remain unchanged.

The standalone skeptical `as if` record now declares `usage_role=fragment`:
its contemporary newspaper example is an independent exclamation, not the
ordinary grammatical conjunction introducing a subordinate clause. The
`talk_to_hand` imperative retains `usage_role=phrase`; the sourced imperative
and its optional continuation do not justify a narrower grammatical role.
Canonical forms, variants, functions, chronology, source notes and examples were
preserved, and no earlier corpus or evaluation-review files were edited.
