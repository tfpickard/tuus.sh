## Temporal interpretation and limitations

Signatures represent an appropriation window in the named kairolect, not a claim about a term's invention or extinction. All fade dates are estimated. The CB window of 1973–1982 is a curation hypothesis around directly attested 1976 popularity. Its onset and fade need independent historical frequency evidence in a subsequent review; no observed early-1980s death is claimed. Some forms remain current in specialist use. These limitations are repeated in the accepted records so downstream tools cannot mistake the parameters for annual measurements.

The Valley window is strongest around 1982. TIME's report of rapid turnover is unusually useful inclusion evidence. Surf-derived formulas have narrower signatures because the source already treats them as displaced; later surf or entertainment reuse is not folded into this particular moment. The yuppie peak is a press-prominence anchor rather than a statistical frequency estimate.

The beginning of the axis has deliberately thin coverage. No words have been invented to fill 1970–1972; runtime should resolve to the nearest supported region and the coverage report should expose that interval.
