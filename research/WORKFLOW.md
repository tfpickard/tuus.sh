# Curation protocol

Candidates require a bounded cultural moment, attested vocabulary, and evidence
of rapid adoption followed by backlash or displacement. First-use evidence is
not peak evidence. Retrospective accounts can establish a moment but cannot
justify precise annual frequencies on their own. Estimated temporal parameters
are curation hypotheses, explicitly qualified in the rationale and confidence.

Researchers return schema-shaped records into staging. Review checks citations,
examples, chronology, membership, function suitability, duplicate variants and
inclusion criteria. Only accepted records enter the runtime artifact. Quotes
must be exact and sourced; constructed examples identify their status.

Low-confidence and culturally borderline candidates remain staged for review.
Reports expose uncovered years and semantic concentrations. Targets are not
permission to pad the corpus.
