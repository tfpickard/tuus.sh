The assigned numerical research target was 35–50 entries. Inclusion evidence did
not justify filling that target. The quiet-work kairolexicon is deliberately thin.

All accepted signatures mark the fade **estimated**. Sources establish usage,
criticism and displacement of a cultural moment, rather than annual frequency
measurements or lexical extinction. Older expressions are included only for their
specified moment-specific sense. These records must not be interpreted as claims
that the expressions were invented at their signature onset.

Constructed examples are labeled `constructed`. Two short NFT glossary quotations
are labeled `attested`; their source reference is explicit. Neutral workplace
examples adapt the attested sense without implying that the exact sentence
occurred in an external source.

## Remaining research limitations

The accepted recent-era material is English-language and weighted toward US/UK
workplace and online communities. It provides weak apologizing coverage and dense
requesting/hyping coverage. The 2024–2026 axis requires explicit fallback or
separately supported material; this file does not conceal that limitation.

In particular, NFT-community greetings continue to circulate. Their signatures
model the bounded speculative expansion and its public reception, with estimated
fade dates. Future curation should seek archived frequency series and independent
lexical-retirement evidence, then revise signatures or retire entries accordingly.

## Final variant-source audit

The exact attested examples and publication dates were rechecked. The two Decrypt
quotations match the inspected source text exactly. The following declared
variants were removed because they were not individually attested in the inspected
sources: “out of an abundance of caution,” “during these uncertain times,”
“frens,” and “you are on mute.” Their canonical forms remain accepted. Ordinary
grammatical expansions can still be connective language without additional corpus
forms. No entries or temporal signatures were removed by this correction.

## Communicative context constraints

Context metadata now restricts finance-specific NFT formulas to relevant investment,
asset, token or verification language. Discord participation and ownership skepticism
require their corresponding community or digital-ownership context. General greetings
and reactions remain unrestricted: GM, GN, fren, ser, WAGMI, looks rare and probably
nothing can accompany ordinary speech acts without asserting financial conditions.

The muted-meeting intervention requires explicit audio or microphone context; merely
scheduling a meeting does not establish that anyone is muted. Supply-chain explanations
require goods, inventory, supply or delivery context. Quiet quitting requires work-boundary
or additional-work context; an ordinary refusal does not imply withdrawal from job duties.
Dates, declared functions and lexical forms were preserved. These constraints narrow
retrieval suitability and do not revise the sources or estimated temporal signatures.

## Context and grammatical-role revision

Risk-qualification language now requires genuine risk, safety or precaution context.
Hustle culture requires work, overwork, ambition or productivity context; unrelated
speaking invitations no longer license invented work commitments. Its concise
constructed example states the ethos rather than supplying a reusable excuse. GN
is a closing, with a short constructed example containing no future promise. Familiar
community addresses and standalone reactions carry their corresponding roles.

A separate contemporary corpus was also revised under explicit ownership authorization.
Technical procedure descriptions and instrument names require synthesis, lexical-tool,
procedure or corpus contexts. Telechronistic requires genuine temporal or historical
context; bare time no longer matches routine punctuality, and on-time contexts are
excluded. A malformed adjective example and plural agreement example were corrected.

Micro-moment keeps its exact primary-source definition and attested plural. Retrieval
requires marketing or a brief communicative-interaction context. Completed shift or
report actions are excluded from being mislabeled as short interactions. Its constructed
example is now a short present request with a fragment label, without an added deadline
or promise. These are conservative semantic-use constraints rather than new dating
claims. Attested quotations, dates, canonical forms and declared functions are unchanged.
