# TμUS project instructions

The project is the Telechronistic μ-Utterance Synthesizer. Typed identifiers,
package names, filenames, paths, environment variables and URLs use ASCII
`tuus` and `u`. Display prose uses TμUS and μ-utterance. The letter is U+03BC
(Greek small letter mu); U+00B5 (micro sign) is forbidden.

Locked taxonomy: telechronistic; kairolect (plural kairolects); kairolexicon
(plural kairolexica); kairolectic; temporal prior; corpus (plural corpora).
Forbid the spellings `chiro`, `corpuses`, and `corpi` outside these instructions.
Never publish a pronunciation guide. Every user-facing word maintains a deadpan
academic register. Never explain the internal premise or recursive corpus.

One input, one variable (the temporal prior), one output. Re-sampling and copying
are actions. No tone controls, accounts, history or social features.

No Vercel hosting, SDK, configuration, storage or analytics. Support identical
builds at root and subpath. Preserve the repository's existing license.

Research agents may own separate era/domain corpus files. Return the shared
schema with real source references and defensible dating rationale. Verify
sources; distinguish estimated dates from observations. Never invent an
attested quotation. Constructed examples must be marked constructed. Terms that
are merely old or still conventional do not qualify by age alone. Exclude hate
terms and slurs; stage borderline cases for Tom. Corpus quality outranks counts.

Do not modify, weaken or delete tests to make them pass. Describe an incorrect
test and propose its correction. No corpus embeddings or large models on the
request path. Keep dates outside semantic embedding text.
